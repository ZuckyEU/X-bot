These cookies grant access to your X account. Only import accounts you own or are authorized to manage. Values are stored privately on the server, not in browser storage.

Where do I find these cookies?
  1. Sign in to the matching account on x.com in your own browser.
  2. Open Developer Tools → Application (or Storage) → Cookies → https://x.com.
  3. Copy the Value of auth_token and ct0. Paste only the values below, not a full cookie header.

Both cookies must come from the same account. The cookies are checked with X before they are saved, so expired or revoked values are rejected.
